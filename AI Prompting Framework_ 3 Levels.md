# AI Prompting Framework

A simple 3-level framework to create better AI prompts.

---

## Level 1: Basic Prompt

### **R-C-T-F**

**R = Role**  
Who should AI act as?

**C = Context**  
What does AI need to know?

**T = Task**  
What should AI do?

**F = Format**  
How should the answer be presented?

**Use when:**  
You need a quick, clear response.

---

## Level 2: Better Prompt

### **R-C-T-F-V-V**

**R = Role**  
Who should AI act as?

**C = Context**  
What does AI need to know?

**T = Task**  
What should AI do?

**F = Format**  
How should the answer be presented?

**V = Verification**  
How should AI check its work?

**V = Validation**  
How should AI test whether the answer is useful and practical?

**Use when:**  
Accuracy, quality, and reliability matter.

---

## Level 3: Expert Prompt

### **R-C-G-T-C-E-F-V-V**

**R = Role**  
Who should AI act as?

**C = Context**  
What does AI need to know?

**G = Goal**  
What outcome do you want?

**T = Task**  
What exactly should AI do?

**C = Constraints**  
What rules, limits, or boundaries must AI follow?

**E = Examples**  
What does good look like?

**F = Format**  
How should the final output be structured?

**V = Verification**  
How should AI check its work?

**V = Validation**  
How should AI test the answer against real-world needs?

**Use when:**  
You need high-quality, repeatable, professional or business-critical results.

---

# Quick Guide

| Level | Framework | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | **R-C-T-F** | Quick everyday prompts |
| **Level 2** | **R-C-T-F-V-V** | Better quality and reliability |
| **Level 3** | **R-C-G-T-C-E-F-V-V** | Complex, professional and business-critical work |

---

## Remember

**Level 1:** Tell AI what to do.  
**Level 2:** Tell AI what to do and check the result.  
**Level 3:** Give AI the complete brief and make it prove the quality.
