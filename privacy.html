1. Who we are

Synottic Prompt Intelligence (the "Service") at prompting.synottic.com is a prompt library and learning platform. It is provided by Synottic, part of Mindaks Global Private Limited, a company incorporated in India ("Synottic", "we", "us").

We are the data controller (called the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) for the personal data described here. Where your organisation gives you access to the Service, we may process some data on its instructions, and your organisation's own privacy notice also applies.

This policy applies to the Service, including its website, sign-in pages and emails. It does not cover third-party websites or tools you open from the Service, such as ChatGPT or Claude.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first and last name, email address, and password. We never store your password itself, only a secure one-way hash of it.
  • Profile details: your function or department, AI proficiency level, and organisation name.
  • Access codes you enter to unlock a programme or organisation library.
  • Your content: prompts you create, save or improve, practice answers, favourites, and feedback you send us.
  • Messages you send to us, for example support or privacy requests.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage within the Service: which prompts you open, copy, test or save, what you search for in the library, and your learning progress. We use this to power features like "recently used" and your progress, and to improve the library.
  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser and device type (user agent), sign-in times, and security events such as failed sign-in attempts.
  • Cookies and local storage: see section 9.
  • Performance data: anonymous page-speed measurements on our public pages, which do not identify you.

Information from others

  • Google or Microsoft, if you choose to sign in with them (see section 3).
  • Your organisation or training provider, if they arrange your access. They may give us your organisation name, programme and the access code linked to you.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to enter, sensitive information such as health data, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, or information about children.

3. Google and Microsoft sign-in

You can create an account or sign in with your Google or Microsoft account. If you do, we ask only for the basic sign-in permissions (openid, email, profile), and we receive:

  • your name,
  • your email address, and whether the provider has verified it, and
  • a unique ID for your account with that provider, so we can recognise you next time.

We do not receive your Google or Microsoft password. We do not get access to your emails, contacts, files, calendar or any other data in those accounts.

We use this information only to create your account, sign you in, keep your account secure, and contact you about your account. We do not use it for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. We do not share it except with the service providers who help us run the Service, as described in section 7.

Synottic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove our access at any time from your Google account settings or your Microsoft account apps page. Removing access stops future sign-ins with that provider. It does not delete your Synottic account. To do that, see section 12.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the Service: create and run your account, show you the right library for your function or programme, save your prompts and progress, and let you sign in.
  • To communicate with you: confirmation, password-reset and account emails, and important notices about the Service or these policies. We do not send marketing emails without your consent, and you can opt out at any time.
  • To keep things secure: prevent fraud and abuse, limit repeated sign-in attempts, and investigate security incidents.
  • To improve the Service: understand which prompts and features are useful, mostly using aggregated or de-identified data.
  • For your organisation's programme: if your access comes through your organisation, we may share information about your enrolment and use of that programme with it (for example, whether you joined and which modules you used).
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Legal bases

Where laws such as the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and improve it, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where we ask for it, for example optional marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, and for other legitimate uses the Act permits. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened, and we may no longer be able to provide the Service to you.

6. AI and your content

  • The Service's feedback and prompt-analysis features run inside our own system. We do not send your prompts or practice answers to outside AI model providers.
  • We do not use your content or personal data to train AI models.
  • Some buttons, such as "Open in ChatGPT" or "Open in Claude", place the prompt text into a link to that third-party service. When you click one, you leave our Service, and that provider's own terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Please do not put confidential information or other people's personal data into prompts you use with any AI tool, unless you are allowed to.

10. How long we keep it

  • Account data and your content: for as long as your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep some of it.
  • Security and sign-in logs: up to 12 months, to protect accounts and investigate abuse.
  • Email confirmation and password-reset links: expire within 24 hours and cannot be used twice.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in secure backups for a short period until they are overwritten.
  • Aggregated or de-identified data that no longer identifies you may be kept to improve the Service.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), one-way hashing of passwords, hashed session tokens, limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

  • Access: get a copy of the personal data we hold about you and a summary of how we use it.
  • Correction: fix data that is wrong or incomplete. You can update most profile details yourself in the app.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account and personal data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Objection and restriction: object to, or ask us to limit, certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India).
  • Complain: raise a grievance with us (see section 14). You can also complain to a regulator, such as your local data protection authority in the EU or the UK Information Commissioner's Office. In India, please contact our Grievance Officer first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can then approach the Data Protection Board of India.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete and correct it; to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" (we do neither); to limit use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect it for inferring characteristics); and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

How to use your rights: email info@synottic.com from the email address on your account, and tell us what you would like. We may need to confirm your identity before acting. We respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. We do not charge for reasonable requests.
